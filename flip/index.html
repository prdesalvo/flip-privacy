ONE MOVE

Turn a word around.

Every letter between the two tiles reverses. Use every letter to make a real word, even if it is not the answer you first had in mind.

ONE DAILY ROUND

A little harder each time.

Start with a one-flip word, then take on a two-flip and a three-flip word. Match the fewest possible flips for a Perfect.

YOUR OWN PACE

See your results and share your score without spoiling the words. Keep the next round close: the Home Screen widget lets you play the whole day without opening the app.
